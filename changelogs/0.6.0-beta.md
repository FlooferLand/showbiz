Welcome to the first beta release!

Showbiz is still in very early access, however you can now rest assured that your world will not combust into a million pieces due to the mod.
Do note that things will most likely still break, be changed, or stop working, especially between major/minor releases like these.

### General
- Added a bitchart for the FNaF 2 toy animatronics
- Added instructions for downloading FFmpeg when you right-click the TV
- Added shader shadow support for bot entities _(required annoyingly porting to `LivingEntity`)_
- Slightly improved the TV texture
- Fixed and greatly improved the spotlights _(switched away from Veil onto IRLights for lighting. You do now have to manually patch shaders with the IRLights menu though)_
- Fixed Wolfman's bowtie being too large/low
- Fixed some bugs with deltatime on wiggle bones (@RedaKarimi)
- Fixed Chica's cupcake being a separate fixture in the `faz` bitchart
- Fixed a bug where the game could crash on first launch due to `showbiz\shows` missing
- Fixed the TV not updating its hanging state
- Fixed FFmpeg 9.0 video info failing to parse due to multiple streams / stream groups
- Fixed edit screens showing a black background when using shaders
- Fixed GeckoLib items moving around in the hotbar
